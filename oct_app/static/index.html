How correction works

      

        Blank reference tiles characterize the repeatable coverslip response.
        The model follows each tile’s slide surface, and its subtraction
        amplitude is capped by the blank references to reduce tissue loss.
      

      

        After Z alignment, the projection averages up to the configured number
        of strong, depth-supported tissue values per pixel. The default is five
        slices. A tissue mask suppresses background; disconnected tissue
        fragments can be excluded.
      

      

        When two coverslips are reliable, their depths constrain the tissue
        search. Otherwise preprocessing continues and reports an error. The
        reference preview is an uncorrected, sampled projection for visual
        review; its candidate blank ranking needs human judgment.
      

      

        Z slice spacing comes from TIFF calibration. Check QC for remaining
        glass and tissue removal, especially where glass and tissue overlap.
